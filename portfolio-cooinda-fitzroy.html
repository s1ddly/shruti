Project information

  • Type: Multi-residential
  • Year: 2023
  • Location: 371 - 385 Gore St, Fitzroy 3065
  • Role: University Studio Project
  • Software used: Revit, Rhino, Adobe Creative Suite

Description

Addressing the issues of speculative housing/lands within a small part of Fitzroy, looking into various land types and researched into what developers find desirable as land for future development. I suggested an alternative strategy towards housing by creating a system that will not allow the exploitation of land use by designing housing funded by a co-orp: proposal of a co-housing community, which consists of scattered mixed tenure apartments, consisting of communal spaces and private apartments. This alternative way of living is aimed towards creating a happy place targeted for women from all walks of life. It seeks to provide a co-living experience, in which people are social and work with each other to create decisions for the benefit of their community.

Scope

- Site analysis: including amenities and services, environmental study, neighbourhood character, urban planning, shadow study and property report
- Design narrative aligned with research, including site analysis and precedent study
- Conceptual design: comprising sketches, diagramming, space planning for apartments
- Documentation: incorporating plans, sections, elevation, 3D model, structure and materiality
- Renders showcasing apartments, communal spaces and shared outdoor spaces
